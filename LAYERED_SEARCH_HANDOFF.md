## Mechanism and boundary

Every search evaluates 4096 final-image windows with the saved 4 qubit/4 layer circuit. Eight descriptors include engineered color/stripe/body/centering cues and supplied target height. Both 28 quantum-angle slots and 8 classical readout slots are optimized; 40 slots are exported with 4 inert final phases frozen at zero. The learned scores, saved threshold and local peak suppression alone define accepted addresses. No target PNG pixels, clean background, inserted coordinates, presence, alpha visibility or template-matcher gate enters default inference. Grover then amplifies the compiled accepted set; its exact Born probabilities supply the sample.

Known striped artwork, known scene identities and supplied size are the domain. Imported target artwork clearly selects the classical comparator. Imported backgrounds are outside the training domain. Circuit inference and Grover are classical simulations; no end-to-end speedup or general person-identification claim is made. Soft gradient fields begin with actual learned scores; smoothing, exposure and visible-time crossfades are display only.
